How to move

  • Run with W A S D. Walk silently with Left Shift.
  • Jump with Space. Hold it to bunny-hop: you jump again the instant you land and keep your speed.
  • Slide: press C while running for a 1.5× speed burst. Slide down ramps to go even faster.
  • Slide-hop: jump during a slide to carry the slide speed into the air.
  • Air strafe: in the air, hold A or D and turn the mouse the same way to gain speed (up to 14 m/s).
  • Ledge climb: hold Space + W against a ledge up to 1.8 m high to pull yourself up.
  • Wall-jump: press G to hold the Snake Pistol, then jump into a wall in the air, up to 3 times before landing.
  • Weapon speed: 1 = Revolver (100%), 2 = Super Pistols (125%), 3 = Dual Heavy MGs (65%).
