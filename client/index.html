Key bindings

Click a key, then press the new key or mouse button. Esc cancels.

How to move

  • Run with W A S D. Walk silently with Left Shift.
  • Jump with Space. Hold it to bunny-hop: you jump again the instant you land and keep your speed.
  • Slide: press C while running for a 1.5× speed burst. Slide down ramps to go even faster.
  • Slide-hop: jump during a slide to carry the slide speed into the air.
  • Air strafe: in the air, hold A or D and turn the mouse the same way to gain speed (up to 14 m/s).
  • Ledge climb: hold Space + W against a ledge up to 1.8 m high to pull yourself up.
  • Switch weapons with 1 (primary), 2 (secondary) and 3 (melee). Each weapon sets your speed: Super Pistols 125%, Dual Heavy MGs 65%.
  • Wall-jump: hold a Snake weapon (key 2 with the default loadout), then jump into a wall in the air, up to 3 times before landing.
  • Shoot with left click, aim down sights with right click, reload with R. Hold Tab for the scoreboard.
  • Rocket-jump: with the Bomber or Rocketeer Pistols, shoot the floor under you as you jump.
  • Armory: pick your loadout and skin rarity, and see every weapon as a hologram.
