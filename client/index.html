Quick Play rooms always have 8 players: bots fill empty places. The map is the one picked on the menu. In a match, press Enter to chat (Y for team chat). Mute or report players from the pause menu.

How to move

  • Run with W A S D. Holding W + A or W + D is faster, like Krunker's strafe sprint.
  • Jump with Space. Hold it to bunny-hop: every hop that leaves the ground the instant you land builds a bit more speed.
  • Slide: press Shift while running. The slide follows your mouse.
  • Slide-hop (the fast way to move): jump, press Shift just before you land, then jump again as you slide. Every chained slide-hop adds speed, up to 16 m/s. Holding Space and Shift together does it automatically.
  • Air control: hold W in the air and turn the mouse to curve your jump without losing speed. A or D plus turning also builds speed (air strafing).
  • Ledge climb: hold Space + W against a ledge up to 1.8 m high.
  • Switch weapons with 1, 2 and 3. Shoot with left click, aim with right click, reload with R, scoreboard on Tab.
  • Wall-jump: hold a Snake weapon (key 2 by default) and jump into a wall in the air, up to 3 times.
  • Rocket-jump: with the Bomber or Rocketeer Pistols, shoot the floor under you as you jump.
